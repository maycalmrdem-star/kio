# Caption — Jensen Huang story

**Title:** From cleaning toilets to a $5 trillion company 🚀

**Caption:**

At 9, he cleaned toilets. At 62, his company became the first ever worth $5 trillion. 🧥

This is the story of Jensen Huang, co-founder and CEO of Nvidia 👇

🎒 Age 9: sent from Asia to the US, he ended up at a reform school in rural Kentucky, where he cleaned the bathrooms.
🍽️ Age 15: dishwasher, busboy and waiter at Denny's.
🎓 Engineering degree from Oregon State, then a master's from Stanford.
☕ 1993: at 30, he planned Nvidia with Chris Malachowsky and Curtis Priem in a Denny's booth in San Jose.
📉 Their first chip flopped, and Nvidia was "30 days from going out of business."
🎮 1999: GeForce 256, "the world's first GPU."
🤖 2006 to today: the bet on CUDA turned gaming chips into the engine of the AI boom.
💰 Oct 2025: Nvidia became the first company ever worth $5 trillion.

Where you start doesn't decide where you end.
