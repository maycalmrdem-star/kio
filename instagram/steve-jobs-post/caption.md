# Caption — Steve Jobs story

**Title:** Apple fired its own founder. Then he saved it. 🍎

**Caption:**

Apple fired its own founder. 12 years later, he came back and saved it 🍎

This is the Steve Jobs story 👇

🏠 1976: after dropping out of college, he started Apple with Steve Wozniak in his parents' garage.
🖋️ A calligraphy class he sat in on later shaped the Mac's fonts.
💰 1980: Apple went public and made him worth over $200 million at 25.
🖥️ 1984: he launched the Macintosh.
🚪 1985: after a boardroom fight, he was pushed out of his own company at 30.
🎬 He started NeXT and bought Pixar, which went on to make Toy Story.
📉 1997: Apple lost $1.04 billion. It bought NeXT for about $400 million, and Jobs came back.
✨ He cut the product line, took $150M from Microsoft and launched the iMac. The next year Apple made a $309M profit.
📱 Then came the iPod, iTunes, the iPhone and the iPad.
🚀 July 2026: Apple briefly topped $5 trillion in value.

"Stay hungry. Stay foolish."
