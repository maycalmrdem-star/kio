# Caption — Bill Gates story

**Title:** He dropped out of Harvard. At 31, he was a billionaire 💻

**Caption:**

He dropped out of Harvard. At 31, he was the youngest billionaire ever at the time 💻

This is how Bill Gates built Microsoft 👇

💾 1975: he and Paul Allen promised software for the Altair 8800 computer, before it existed. They coded day and night for two months to build it.
🎓 It worked. "Micro-Soft" was born in Albuquerque, and Gates left Harvard at 19 to run it.
🖥️ 1981: IBM needed an operating system. Microsoft bought QDOS, turned it into MS-DOS, and kept the right to sell it to other PC makers too.
🪟 1985: the first Windows. 1986: IPO at $21 a share. 1987: billionaire at 31.
🌍 2008: he stepped back from Microsoft to focus on the Gates Foundation: health, vaccines and education.
📈 $1,000 at the 1986 IPO grew to about $5.5 million. Microsoft is now worth around $3.9 trillion.
