# Caption — How AI works (explainer)

**Title:** How AI actually works, in 8 slides 🧠

**Caption:**

Most people use AI every day. Almost nobody knows how it works 🧠

Here's the simple version 👇

🍳 An algorithm is just a recipe: clear steps that turn an input into an answer.
💻 Classic code follows rules a human wrote. AI learns the rules from millions of examples.
🔄 How it learns: guess, measure the error, adjust, then repeat billions of times.
🤖 ChatGPT doesn't think. It predicts the next word, again and again. GPT-3 alone had 175 billion parameters.
📱 Your feed is a prediction too: watch time, rewatches, likes, shares and skips teach it what keeps you scrolling.
💰 And it costs a fortune: Big Tech plans about $725B of AI spending in 2026, up from $410B in 2025.

AI isn't magic. It's data + math + chips.
