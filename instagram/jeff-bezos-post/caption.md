# Caption — Jeff Bezos story

**Title:** From a garage bookstore to a $2.9 trillion company 📦

**Caption:**

He quit Wall Street to sell books online. Now Amazon is worth about $2.9 trillion 📦

This is how Jeff Bezos built Amazon 👇

🚗 1994: he quit a top Wall Street job, drove from New York to Seattle writing the business plan, and started Amazon in a rented garage.
💰 He told his parents they'd probably lose their money and gave it about a 30% chance. They invested about $245,000 anyway.
📚 1995: Amazon opened online as a bookstore. 1997: it went public.
📉 2000–2001: the dot-com crash took the stock from $106 to $6, a drop of more than 90%. He kept building.
☁️ Then came the real money machines: Prime (2005), AWS cloud (2006) and Kindle (2007).
🚀 He bought The Washington Post, stepped down as CEO in 2021, and flew to space with Blue Origin.

"Minimize regret, not risk." At 80, would he regret not trying? That question made him quit.
