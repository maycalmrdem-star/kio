# Caption — Mark Zuckerberg story

**Title:** He said no to $1 billion at 22 💵

**Caption:**

At 22, he said no to $1 billion. Today 3.6 billion people use his apps every day 🌍

This is the Mark Zuckerberg story 👇

💻 2004: he launched TheFacebook from his Harvard dorm room.
🚫 He dropped out and moved to Palo Alto. Peter Thiel invested $500,000 for 10.2%.
💵 2006: Yahoo reportedly offered about $1 billion. Almost everyone wanted to sell. He refused.
📸 2012: Facebook went public and bought Instagram for $1B, when it had just 13 employees and no revenue.
💬 2014: WhatsApp for $19B.
🥽 2021: Facebook became Meta, betting on VR, smart glasses and AI.
📈 2026: 3.6 billion daily users across Meta's apps, and a company worth around $1.6 trillion.

Saying no can be worth trillions.
