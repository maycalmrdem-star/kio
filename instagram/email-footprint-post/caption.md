**Title:** Hackers Don't Break In — They Log In 🔑

**Caption:**

Most people make this mistake with their email 🤦

Same email for every app. Your real full name on every account. The same username for years. And sometimes… giving your main login to someone you don't even know.

Real hackers don't work like in the movies 🎬
No green code. No 10-second hack.
They quietly search for the small details you left behind (an old post, a birthday, your pet's name, an email in a data leak) 🕵️ and connect the dots 🧩

Protect yourself 🛡️
✅ Use a second email for sign-ups & giveaways
✅ Don't put your full real name everywhere
✅ Never share your main login, not even with "support"
✅ Turn on two-factor authentication (2FA)
✅ Check if your email was leaked: haveibeenpwned.com

Hackers don't break in. They log in. 🔑
