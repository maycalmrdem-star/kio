# Caption — Sam Altman / OpenAI story

**Title:** From a nonprofit to $852 billion 🤖

**Caption:**

OpenAI started as a nonprofit. In 2026 it's valued at $852 billion 🤖

This is the Sam Altman story 👇

📍 Age 19: he left Stanford to build Loopt, a location-sharing app. It sold in 2012 for about $43 million, roughly what investors had put in.
🌱 2014: at 28, he became president of Y Combinator, the startup program behind Airbnb, Stripe and Dropbox.
🤖 2015: he co-founded OpenAI as a nonprofit. AI turned out to be hugely expensive, so in 2019 it added a for-profit arm and Microsoft invested $1B.
💬 Nov 30, 2022: ChatGPT launched, reached about 100 million users in roughly two months, and passed 900 million weekly users in 2026.
🚪 Nov 2023: the board fired him. Most employees threatened to quit, and within days he was back.
💰 March 2026: a $122B funding round, the biggest in Silicon Valley history, at an $852B valuation.

Your first startup doesn't have to win.
