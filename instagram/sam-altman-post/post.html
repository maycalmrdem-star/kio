Chapter 1Age 19

He left Stanford
to build an app.

His first startup, Loopt, let friends share their location on their phones. In 2012 it sold for about $43 million, roughly what investors had put in. Not a huge win, but a start.

Chapter 22014

He ran the world’s
top startup school.

At 28, he became president of Y Combinator, the startup program behind companies like Airbnb, Stripe and Dropbox. He learned what makes startups explode.

Chapter 32015

A nonprofit to
build safe AI.

He co-founded OpenAI with Elon Musk, Ilya Sutskever, Greg Brockman and others as a nonprofit. Building AI turned out to be hugely expensive, so in 2019 it added a for-profit arm, and Microsoft invested $1 billion.
