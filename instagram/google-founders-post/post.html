Meet the founders of Google

Two students.
One search engine.

Larry PageBorn 1973, Michigan
Sergey BrinBorn 1973, Moscow. Moved to the US at 6

They met at Stanford in 1995 as PhD students. At first, they argued about almost everything.

Chapter 11996

A smarter way
to rank the web.

Their project, BackRub, ranked pages by how many other sites linked to them. They renamed it after “googol”: a 1 followed by 100 zeros.

riyad__31mh·Swipe →
Chapter 21998

“Deposit it when
you get one.”

Sun co-founder Andy Bechtolsheim wrote them a $100,000 check made out to “Google Inc.” The company didn’t exist and had no bank account yet. They set up in a garage in Menlo Park.

riyad__31mh·Swipe →
Chapter 31999

They almost
sold it.

They offered Google to Excite, a big search engine back then, for about $1 million. Reportedly an investor even talked them down to $750,000. Excite said no.

Chapter 4The money machine
Googleplex
Photo: Austin McKinley · CC BY 3.0

Search is free.
Ads pay for it.

Small text ads next to search results turned into one of the biggest businesses on Earth. In 2004, Google went public at $85 a share.
