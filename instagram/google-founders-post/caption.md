# Caption — Google founders story

**Title:** They tried to sell Google for $1 million 🔍

**Caption:**

They tried to sell Google for about $1 million. Today it's worth more than $4 trillion 🔍

This is the story of Larry Page and Sergey Brin 👇

🎓 1995: two Stanford PhD students meet, and argue about almost everything.
🔍 1996: their project BackRub ranks web pages by how many sites link to them. They rename it after "googol", a 1 followed by 100 zeros.
💵 1998: Andy Bechtolsheim writes a $100,000 check to "Google Inc." before the company even exists. "Deposit it when you get one." They move into a garage in Menlo Park.
🤔 1999: they offer Google to Excite for about $1 million, reportedly talked down to $750,000. Excite says no.
💰 Search stays free, and ads pay for it. 2004: Google goes public at $85 a share.
📺 2006: YouTube for $1.65B. 2015: Alphabet is born.
📈 2026: Alphabet crosses $4 trillion.

The deal you lose can be your luckiest.
