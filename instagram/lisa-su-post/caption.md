# Caption — Lisa Su story

**Title:** She took over a $2 stock. Now AMD is worth $1 trillion 🚀

**Caption:**

When Lisa Su took over, AMD's stock was under $2–3 and the company was losing money. In September 2026, AMD crossed $1 trillion in value 🚀

This is how one engineer saved AMD 👇

👩‍🔬 Three degrees from MIT, including a PhD in electrical engineering, then years making chips faster at Texas Instruments, IBM and Freescale.
📉 October 2014: she became CEO of a company losing money and losing ground to Intel.
💡 She kept funding one big bet, a new chip design called Zen, even in AMD's weakest years.
💻 2017: Zen launched as Ryzen, and AMD was back in the game.
🖥️ EPYC server chips, the $49B Xilinx deal (2022) and a huge AI chip deal with OpenAI (2025).
🏆 TIME's CEO of the Year 2023, and the first woman to run a Fortune 500 semiconductor company.
📈 Sept 2026: a $615 record share price and a $1 trillion market value.

Fix the product. The money follows.
